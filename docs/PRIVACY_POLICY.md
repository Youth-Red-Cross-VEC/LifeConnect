# LifeConnect privacy policy draft

The college's authorized data owner must approve this draft, its retention periods, and the contact channel before publication.

LifeConnect helps the college's Youth Red Cross coordinate blood donation requests, contact eligible donors, and record donation outcomes.

| Information | How LifeConnect uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Donor identity, contact details, date of birth, address, blood group, health information provided during enrollment, and donation history | Manage registration, support donor eligibility and availability, match requests, notify donors, and generate certificates |
| Patient and attendant details supplied on a blood request, including contact, hospital, blood group and request details | Coordinate a request, verify its operational status, and record the outcome. Only submit information you are authorized to provide. |
| Support messages and account, session, security, and audit records | Respond to enquiries, maintain accounts, prevent abuse, and investigate incidents |

Authorized coordinators and service operators access information needed for their duties. As the service requires, limited information may be shared with hospitals or participating donors to coordinate a request. Hosting, email, Maps and reCAPTCHA providers may process the information necessary to deliver their services. The data owner must confirm and document the actual providers, locations and disclosures before publishing this notice.

LifeConnect uses password hashes, restricted account access, session controls, rate limiting and HTTPS in its production configuration. Operational records such as donation unit totals and outcomes may be retained after identifying donor details are erased. Retention periods, exceptions and backup handling are described in [Data retention](DATA_RETENTION.md). Backups may retain earlier copies until expiry and are handled separately when restored.

Donors review the registration terms before submitting a profile. The data owner must confirm which processing activities rely on consent and explain how it can be withdrawn. Donors can request access or corrections, and can request erasure under **Account privacy** after verifying their current password. A super administrator reviews the request. An approved request ends existing sessions. For patient records, anonymous submissions or accounts you cannot access, use the verified contact process below. The review may consider records that must be preserved for an approved operational or legal hold.

The college must publish an authorized contact address, responsible team and response timeline here before release. Contact the designated channel for privacy questions, corrections, access requests, erasure, or complaints. The team should explain how to verify identity securely. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, identity numbers or medical records to an unverified mailbox.
